Happy Birthday, My Love ❤️

Happy Birthday to the girl who somehow managed to make an arranged meeting feel like absolute destiny. I don't know what magic you carry, but from our very first conversation, it never felt like I was talking to someone I'd just met. It felt comfortable... effortless... like we'd known each other for years.

Now let's address the most important thing... You're shy. Very shy. Until you're not. 😂 And then suddenly I wonder if there's a "pause" button somewhere, because the girl who replied in one word has now started giving TED Talks! And honestly... I wouldn't have it any other way.

"That one shy glance at your house... my heart quietly said, 'Yep... this is her.'"

Then comes your favourite hobby — being professionally stubborn. Full attitude, zero flexibility, arguing on principle even when you know I'm right! 😄 But give it five minutes, and suddenly you're listening properly, understanding everything, and ending up right anyway. It's you refusing to agree until you've fully understood it — and that, annoyingly, is one of the most attractive things about you.

You're kind. You're caring. You're funny without even trying. You're my peace after a long day. I promise to keep making fun of your stubbornness, to listen to your endless stories, to support every dream you chase, and to love you exactly the way you are. No upgrades. No changes. No "new version." Because this version of Kirti is already my absolute favourite.

Forever yours,
Bhanu ❤️
